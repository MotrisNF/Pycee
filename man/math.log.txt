NAME
    math.log - logarithms and the exponential: exp, log, log2, log10

SYNOPSIS
    math.exp(x)   math.log(x)   math.log(x, base)   math.log2(x)
    math.log10(x)

DESCRIPTION
    exp(x) is e to the power x. log(x) is the natural logarithm (base
    e); log(x, base) in any base; log2 and log10 in base 2 and 10. All
    give floats. x must be > 0, and base > 0 and not 1.

ERRORS
    ValueError      x <= 0, or a wrong base.
    OverflowError   exp of a number too large (exp(1000)).

EXAMPLES
    >>> import math
    >>> math.log(math.e)
    1.0
    >>> math.log(8, 2)
    3.0
    >>> math.log10(1000)
    3.0
    >>> math.log(0)
    ValueError: log() needs a number > 0
