# IndexError
NAME
    IndexError - an index out of range

DESCRIPTION
    Raised by l[i], s[i] and t[i] when i is not between -len and len - 1,
    by pop() of an empty list, and by random.choice of an empty
    sequence. Slices never raise it: their bounds are clipped.

EXAMPLES
    >>> [1, 2, 3][3]
    IndexError: list index 3 out of range (length 3)
    >>> [].pop()
    IndexError: pop from an empty list
