# max list.max tuple.max set.max
NAME
    max - the largest item: max(x), or x.max() on a list, tuple or set

SYNOPSIS
    max(iterable)
    max(expression for (x in iterable) ...)
    l.max()

DESCRIPTION
    The largest item, compared with >: numbers, strings (by bytes) or
    objects with compare(); all of the same type. The first of several
    equal ones. Takes anything a for walks, or a comprehension alone
    inside.

ERRORS
    ValueError    nothing to compare.
    TypeError     items that cannot be compared with each other.

EXAMPLES
    >>> max([3, 1, 2])
    3
    >>> max("pycee")
    "y"
    >>> max([1, "a"])
    TypeError: cannot compare 'string' with 'number'
