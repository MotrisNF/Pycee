# math.sin math.cos math.tan math.asin math.acos math.atan math.atan2 math.sinh math.cosh math.tanh math.degrees math.radians math.hypot
NAME
    math.sin - trigonometry: sin, cos, tan, their inverses, hyperbolic
    ones, angle conversion and hypot

SYNOPSIS
    math.sin(x)   math.cos(x)   math.tan(x)
    math.asin(x)  math.acos(x)  math.atan(x)  math.atan2(y, x)
    math.sinh(x)  math.cosh(x)  math.tanh(x)
    math.degrees(x)   math.radians(x)   math.hypot(x, y)

DESCRIPTION
    Angles are in radians (pi radians are 180 degrees); degrees(x) and
    radians(x) convert. asin and acos take x from -1 to 1. atan2(y, x)
    is the angle of the point (x, y), with the right quadrant (from -pi
    to pi). hypot(x, y) is the length of (x, y): sqrt(x*x + y*y), without
    overflowing on the way. All give floats.

ERRORS
    ValueError    x out of the domain (asin(2), sin(inf)).

EXAMPLES
    >>> import math
    >>> math.cos(0)
    1.0
    >>> math.degrees(math.atan2(1, -1))
    135.0
    >>> math.hypot(3, 4)
    5.0
