# string.strip string.lstrip string.rstrip
NAME
    string.strip - the string without spaces (or other characters) at
    its ends: strip, lstrip, rstrip

SYNOPSIS
    s.strip()     s.lstrip()     s.rstrip()
    s.strip(chars)   s.lstrip(chars)   s.rstrip(chars)

DESCRIPTION
    strip takes away the white space (spaces, tabs, new lines) at both
    ends; lstrip only at the left (the start), rstrip only at the right
    (the end). Nothing in the middle is touched.

    With chars (a string), they take away any of those characters
    instead, however many and in any order: "xxhixx".strip("x") is "hi".

    rstrip("\n") is how to take the "\n" off a line read from a file.

EXAMPLES
    >>> "  hello  ".strip()
    "hello"
    >>> "  hello  ".lstrip()
    "hello  "
    >>> "line\n".rstrip("\n")
    "line"
    >>> "--==title==--".strip("-=")
    "title"
