# signal.SIGINT signal.SIGTERM signal.SIGHUP signal.SIGQUIT signal.SIGUSR1 signal.SIGUSR2 signal.SIGALRM signal.SIGPIPE signal.SIGCHLD
NAME
    signal.SIGINT - the signals the module handles, by name

DESCRIPTION
    Ints with Linux's numbers:
        SIGINT    2   Ctrl+C (without a handler: KeyboardInterrupt)
        SIGQUIT   3   Ctrl+\
        SIGHUP    1   the terminal was closed
        SIGTERM  15   "please end" (kill's default)
        SIGUSR1  10   free for the program's own use
        SIGUSR2  12   free for the program's own use
        SIGALRM  14   a timer
        SIGPIPE  13   writing to a pipe nobody reads
        SIGCHLD  17   a child process ended
    No other signal can be handled: SIGKILL and SIGSTOP cannot be by
    anyone, and SIGSEGV, SIGFPE, SIGBUS and SIGILL are not allowed,
    since going on after one would not be memory safe.
